Team Dashboard — Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026

Team Dashboard is built to work entirely in your browser. This page explains exactly what data exists, where it goes, and who can see it.

What Team Dashboard stores

The only data the app holds is what you paste or type in: team names, and for each team a list of work items — a completed date, an optional start date, and an optional work type — plus the shared settings (labels, the text that marks unplanned work, the work-type filter list) and your preferences (theme, chosen team, date window). No account is required to use the app, and there is no analytics, tracking or advertising of any kind.

Note what is not stored: no ticket IDs, no ticket titles, no names of people. The app only ever needs dates and a type.

Where that data lives

In your browser (always). Everything is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. This is the only copy that exists if you never sign in.

In Google Firebase (only if you sign in). Signing in with Google is entirely optional and exists for one reason: seeing the same teams on your other devices. If you do, a copy of your teams and settings is written to a Firestore database in a Firebase project owned by the app's author.

Who can see your data if you sync

Your data is stored in a single document whose name is your Firebase authentication ID. The database's security rules allow read and write access to that document only from the signed-in account it belongs to:

allow read, write: if request.auth != null && request.auth.uid == uid;

No other user of the app can read your teams, and there is deliberately no shared-workspace or multi-user editing model. The rules are kept in the repository as firestore.rules so you can check them.

The app's author has administrative access to the Firebase project, as the owner of any database necessarily does. That access is not used to look at anyone's data.

What Google receives

If you sign in, Google handles the sign-in itself and knows you have used this app, in the same way it knows about any other site you sign in to with a Google account. The app receives only your email address and display name, which are used to show who is signed in — they are not stored in the database alongside your teams.

What is never sent anywhere

  • Nothing at all leaves your browser unless you sign in.
  • There is no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no cookies beyond what Google's sign-in itself sets.
  • The page loads no third-party code except Firebase's own SDK from Google's CDN, and only when sync is switched on. A Content-Security-Policy on the page restricts network access to that short list.

Deleting your data

  • Local copy: "Clear this team's data" on the Your Data tab empties one team; deleting a team removes it entirely. Clearing your browser's site data removes everything.
  • Synced copy: deleting your teams while signed in replaces the cloud copy with the empty one, on every device.
  • Everything, permanently: email eagleadams86@gmail.com and your stored document will be deleted from Firestore.

Changes to this policy

If what the app stores or where it sends it ever changes, this page and its effective date change in the same release.
